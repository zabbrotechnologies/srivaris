HOW WE WORK

Our Engineering Process

A structured, transparent methodology from initial consultation through full system go-live and ongoing support.

01
Discovery & Site Audit
Deep-dive assessment of your production line, bottlenecks, safety needs, and automation potential to define scope.
02
Solution Design & BOM
Custom engineering schematics, PLC logic flowcharts, robot cell layout, and detailed bill of materials prepared.
03
Build & Integration
Hardware assembly, panel wiring, robot programming, HMI development, and full system integration in our workshop.
04
Testing & Commissioning
Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT), Site Acceptance Testing (SAT), operator training, and final calibration on your floor.
05
Go-Live & Support
Full production go-live, remote monitoring setup, AMC agreement, and 24/7 technical helpdesk activation.
OUR FOUNDATION

6 Strategic Pillars of Impact

Every system engineered by Srivaris is built around these core principles.

1. Improving Efficiency

Automating repetitive cycles, eliminating throughput bottlenecks, and maximizing overall equipment utilization.

2. Enhancing Productivity

Accelerating line speeds with high-speed robotics, precise motion timing, and zero human fatigue errors.

3. Ensuring Reliability

Designing fault-tolerant control architectures that guarantee high continuous plant uptime.

4. Enabling Innovation

Deploying adaptive AI vision, flexible EOAT tooling, and modular factory architectures.

5. Digital Transformation

Connecting physical shop-floor assets to MES/ERP cloud dashboards for real-time telemetry analytics.

6. Enhancing Safety

Shielding personnel from hazardous tasks through cobot safety zones, light curtains, and fail-safe logic.
